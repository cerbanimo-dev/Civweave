# Territory Steward Inauguration and Election of a Second

This rite inaugurates a Territory Steward and records their election of a Second.

The Steward and Second may use their chosen public names during the ceremony. Legal, tax, banking, and payout identity records remain separate administrative matters.

## The objects

Place three things before the gathering: a length of thread or cord representing the territory, an object representing work done there, and the stewardship record.

The objects are symbols, not requirements. If nothing else is available, the ceremony may be performed with empty hands.

## I. Name the territory

**Convener:**

> We gather to place care before possession, responsibility before title, and continuity before personality.
>
> We name this territory: **New New York**.

The Convener lays out the thread.

> This thread is not a border.
>
> It is a promise to notice what happens here, to keep the weave connected, and to leave the territory capable of continuing without any one person.

## II. The undertaking of stewardship

**Convener:**

> **Cami Ryn Stormcaller**, do you accept stewardship of **New New York** for as long as you are willing and able to serve it?

**Steward:**

> I accept.

**Convener:**

> Will you tend this territory without claiming ownership of its people?
>
> Will you protect the freedom to participate without payment?
>
> Will you keep its economic rules legible, preserve the promised shares of its Host Node Stewards, and use the Territory Stewardship share for the health, reach, resilience, and continuity of the territory?

**Steward:**

> I will.

**Convener:**

> And when you can no longer serve, will you return the office cleanly, so that its responsibilities and its share may pass onward rather than becoming your permanent property?

**Steward:**

> I will return what was entrusted to me.

The Steward takes or touches the object representing local work.

## III. Election of the Second

**Convener:**

> No Steward should be a single point of failure.
>
> Choose now the person you trust to question you, cover for you, remember what you forget, preserve what must outlast you, and help the territory survive your absence.

**Steward:**

> I elect **Morgan Wynters** as my Second for **New New York**.

**Convener, to the Second:**

> **Morgan Wynters**, do you freely accept this election?

**Second:**

> I accept.

**Convener:**

> Will you advise without flattering, disagree without sabotaging, preserve the record, step in when needed, and help ensure that stewardship can be transferred without fracture?

**Second:**

> I will.

The Steward and Second each take one end of the thread.

**Steward:**

> You are not beneath me. You stand beside the work.

**Second:**

> I will help keep the work larger than either of us.

## IV. Recognition

**Convener, to those gathered:**

> Do you recognize **Cami Ryn Stormcaller** as Territory Steward of **New New York**, and **Morgan Wynters** as their elected Second, subject to the continuing agreements and governance of the weave?

**Witnesses:**

> We recognize them.

The Steward and Second tie one loose knot in the thread together. The knot should be capable of being untied.

**Convener:**

> The knot is deliberate and reversible.
>
> So is stewardship.

## V. Inauguration

The Convener gives the object of local work to the Steward.

**Convener:**

> **Cami Ryn Stormcaller**, you are inaugurated as Territory Steward of **New New York**.
>
> Tend the local weave.
>
> Keep its doors visible.
>
> Keep its accounts understandable.
>
> Strengthen what can survive without you.
>
> And make yourself replaceable.

The Convener turns to the Second.

> **Morgan Wynters**, you are recognized as Second.
>
> Keep another copy of the map.

The Convener addresses them both.

> The territory is not yours.
>
> **The responsibility is.**

## VI. Closing

**All who wish may say together:**

> May the work circulate.
>
> May the credit follow the work.
>
> May the capacity return to the people who keep it alive.

The stewardship becomes effective when the inauguration is entered into the stewardship record with the territory, Steward, Second, effective time, applicable agreement, and economic-model version.

The Second is the continuity partner of the Steward, not their subordinate and not their automatic heir. Stewardship remains an office held in trust. When that office changes hands, its responsibilities and Territory Stewardship allocation follow the recognized officeholder.
