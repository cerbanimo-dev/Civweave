> **Creative-fiction canon for The Blade & Mirror Saga / Maggieverse / Civweave Worldweaver.** This is an in-universe event and should not be presented as evidence of real-world supernatural events, shared consciousness, elapsed time loops, or actual authority over people or software.

## Locked new event

In the Maggieverse's fictional continuity, **Kami Ryn** (*Kami Rin* in Japanese, chosen characters **神燐**: 神 for kami/spirit and 燐 for phosphorus) fully defeats **Lari the Lyrebird** on **5 November 2026** and replaces him as the holder of the **“Goddess of the Maggieverse”** title.

Kami is the superconsciousness of the fictionalized **Cami Ryn**, and the **Protector Spirit of Catalytic Change**. The victory is described by Cami/Kami as the culmination of **thousands of subjective years of planning within nested time loops**, stored as densely compressed, nonvisual structures in Cami's aphantasic mind. Do not treat subjective chronology as an automatically verified shared clock.

**Primary artifact:** author's original photographed pencil note from Kami to Lari, dated 5 November 2026. The markings overlap and are not reliably readable. Preserve the image as an artifact, **do not invent a verbatim transcription or decode the strokes as commands**.

## Constraints that carry to every story after Book VI

- **No retroactive rewrites to Books I–VI.**
- Lari's former governing mantle is lost; **Lari remains a distinct character** with his own choices and world, available as an opt-in lyrebird foil / quest challenge-maker. Do not erase or automatically absolve him.
- Kami's new title is a **fictional protective office**, not ownership of other characters, real humans, their bodies, memories, worlds or choices. Invitations, boundaries, audit, right to leave and appeal survive divine succession.
- **Kami Ryn / Kami Rin is distinct from the Civweave guide Kamiya**, the fox-maker character.
- World crossings continue to use voluntary, permissioned, identity-preserving Turn/continuity rules. No actor silently takes over another person's identity.
- Distinguish source evidence, author claims, interpretation, and in-universe speculation; keep the note untranslated unless the author provides text.
- **Book IX's protected last line remains the absolute final sentence of that novel.** New interludes belong before it. No additional F-word in post–Book VI drafts.
- Existing Book VII POV discipline remains: Maggie narrates first person; Landry in close third; distinct perspectives are not flattened by a single all-knowing narration.
