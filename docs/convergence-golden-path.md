# Convergence golden path

The convergence release protects one cross-realm loop above all other breadth.

1. Configure a local or user-supplied model, or continue in deterministic mode.
2. Enter Commonweave.
3. Tell Weaveling a wish.
4. Review the generated weave and its assumptions.
5. Activate the intention explicitly.
6. Open the required Living School path.
7. Complete learning content and assessment evidence.
8. Hand demonstrated capability to Cerbanimo.
9. Complete a work unit and attach evidence.
10. Validate completion through the canonical deterministic and model-assisted boundary.
11. Append Skill XP, Acorns, and Buttons to the signed reward ledger.
12. Open FellowFare for a required material, offer, or exchange proposal.
13. Review and explicitly publish or accept the exchange action.
14. Open Anarchadia Passport.
15. Derive balances, skills, intentions, and receipts from canonical records.
16. Return to Commonweave with progress preserved.
17. Continue, revise, or complete the intention.

Every convergence wave must identify which of these steps it touches and exercise those steps on the installed PWA, not only through a historical browser entry.
