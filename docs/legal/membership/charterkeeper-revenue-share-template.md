# Charterkeeper revenue-share agreement — draft

> Execution draft only. This document is not effective until reviewed for the actual relationship and jurisdiction, incorporated into the applicable Civweave agreements, and accepted by authorized parties.

## Parties and role

This agreement is between Cerbanimo LLC / Civweave (the **Platform**) and the operator of an approved Civweave source Guild acting as a **Charterkeeper**.

The Charterkeeper helps establish a specified child Guild through either a founder-transfer route or a mentor-direct route. Day-to-day authority belongs to the child Guild and its appointed Guildkeeper after handoff. Charterkeeper status does not make the Charterkeeper the owner, employer, franchisor, merchant of record, or perpetual controller of the child Guild merely because the Charterkeeper helped establish it.

## Activation

No Charterkeeper revenue share begins merely because a person is nominated. The required training must be completed and the child Guild must independently accept the Charter relationship using its registered Civweave Guild identity.

Only one Charterkeeper relationship may be active for a child Guild at a time. A Charter may be ended by the Charterkeeper source Guild or the child Guild under the product rules and final agreement.

## Revenue share

The Charterkeeper share is paid by the Platform from the Cerbanimo-side amount that would otherwise remain with the Platform for the applicable transaction. It is not an additional charge to a member or customer and does not reduce the system/compute reserve or the child Guildkeeper's established share.

The staging v1 policy allocates 50% of the existing Cerbanimo-side amount for an eligible child-Guild transaction to the active Charterkeeper relationship. The other 50% remains on the Cerbanimo side and may be further allocated under separately applicable Platform policies, including Territory Stewardship.

This is a one-hop contractual share. Charterkeepers do not receive recursive commissions from Guilds chartered by their child Guilds, and no multi-level or downline compensation right is created.

## Continued service requirement

The Charterkeeper revenue share depends on the Charterkeeper continuing to operate the source Guild identified by the Charter. Civweave may verify the source Guild's registered operator identity before each settlement. If the source Guild is closed, transferred to another operator, deregistered, or otherwise no longer controlled by the original Charterkeeper, future Charterkeeper revenue may stop without affecting prior valid settlements.

## Payout method

When Stripe Connect is used, the Charterkeeper share may be transferred to the connected account belonging to the Charterkeeper's active source Guild. A Charter record does not authorize arbitrary third-party payout destinations.

Amounts may accrue as accounting obligations while agreement, identity, tax, payout, fraud, dispute, or other compliance gates are incomplete. Cash transfer occurs only when the Platform's payout requirements are satisfied.

## Refunds, disputes and reversals

If an underlying transaction is refunded, disputed, reversed, charged back, or otherwise reduced, the Platform may proportionally reverse, offset, or net the related Charterkeeper share to the extent permitted by law and the final agreement. Each adjustment must remain linked to the original source transaction.

## No ownership or equity

A Charterkeeper revenue share is compensation for qualifying Charterkeeper services. It does not grant equity in Cerbanimo LLC, ownership of Civweave, ownership of the child Guild, a partnership interest, a franchise right, or a right to control the child Guild after handoff.

## Compliance

Before live payouts, the Platform must complete any required identity, tax, classification, payment-provider, labor, licensing, reporting, withholding, consumer-protection, franchise/business-opportunity, and other applicable reviews for the actual Charterkeeper relationship and jurisdiction.
