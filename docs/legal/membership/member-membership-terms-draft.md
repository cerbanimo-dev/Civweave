# Civweave paid membership terms — draft

> Execution draft for product, accounting, and legal review. These membership-specific terms are not effective until incorporated into reviewed Civweave Terms of Service and activated through the legal release process.

## 1. Seller and membership service

A paid Civweave membership is a recurring service sold by Cerbanimo LLC through Civweave (together, **Civweave** for purposes of these membership terms). The member purchases one Civweave membership from Civweave. The selected Guild and its Guildkeeper provide community capacity and related Guild services within Civweave, but the Guildkeeper is not the seller or merchant of record for the Civweave membership solely because that Guild is selected.

Payment accepted by Civweave or its payment processor satisfies the member's payment obligation for the applicable Civweave membership cycle. The member does not owe a separate portion of that same membership fee directly to the selected Guildkeeper.

## 2. Recurring billing

Unless checkout states otherwise, paid membership renews monthly at the price shown before purchase. By starting a paid membership, the member authorizes Civweave and its payment processor to charge the selected payment method for each renewal until cancellation takes effect.

Civweave will show the applicable tier and recurring price at checkout. Taxes, if Civweave is required to collect them, may be added or included as stated at checkout.

A price change applies only as permitted by applicable law and the broader Civweave Terms. Required advance notice and consent will be provided before a changed recurring charge takes effect.

## 3. Cancellation

Unless a different legally required rule applies, a member may cancel an automatically renewing membership so that cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. Access and paid membership benefits continue through that period unless a refund, dispute, account restriction, or other applicable rule requires a different result.

The final Terms and production interface must provide the cancellation method, any required statutory cancellation language, and any jurisdiction-specific timing or notice rights before paid memberships are enabled for live customers.

## 4. Guildkeeper revenue share

Civweave may pay the selected Guildkeeper a contractual share of Civweave membership revenue as compensation for Guild capacity and related Guild services. That payment obligation runs from Civweave to the Guildkeeper under a separate Guildkeeper agreement.

For the current economic policy, after payment-processing fees are determined, membership service net is allocated 50% to the system / compute reserve, 25% to the Guildkeeper revenue share, and 25% to Cerbanimo. The allocation may be changed prospectively only under the applicable Civweave Terms, Guildkeeper agreement, and law.

A Guildkeeper payout is not a separate debt owed by the member. Civweave does not describe or treat the member's membership payment as money held in escrow for the Guildkeeper. A delayed, failed, suspended, or reversed Guildkeeper payout does not by itself cancel or invalidate an otherwise paid member's membership.

## 5. Membership credits

Any compute credits, neurons, or similar service entitlements included with membership are rights to use eligible Civweave services under the applicable product rules. They are not cash, deposits, escrowed funds, bank balances, stored monetary value, or promises of fiat redemption.

Unless a separately reviewed product rule expressly provides otherwise, membership service credits cannot be withdrawn as cash or transferred outside Civweave. Any proposal to make them redeemable, withdrawable, or generally transferable requires a separate legal and payments review before launch.

## 6. Refunds, disputes, and failed payments

Civweave is responsible for customer-facing administration of the membership transaction, including any refund, credit, cancellation, or dispute treatment owed under the broader Civweave Terms or applicable law.

If a payment fails or is reversed, membership access or benefits may be limited after any legally required notice or cure period. A payment dispute or refund may also cause corresponding accounting adjustments between Civweave and the Guildkeeper without creating a new payment obligation for the member.

## 7. Guild changes and availability

Selecting a Guild associates the membership with that Guild for applicable capacity and revenue-share purposes. Civweave may provide a process to change Guilds or recover from an unavailable Guild. A Guildkeeper's payout status, connected payment account status, or inability to receive a payout does not by itself terminate the member's paid relationship with Civweave.

The final Terms must define any migration, reassignment, service interruption, or prorating rules that apply when a selected Guild closes or becomes unavailable.

## 8. Relationship to the Civweave Terms

These membership terms supplement the broader Civweave Terms of Service. The final reviewed Terms must govern privacy, acceptable use, account suspension, limitations of liability, dispute resolution, governing law, intellectual property, statutory consumer rights, and other platform-wide subjects not fully addressed here.

Before production activation, legal review must address the actual launch jurisdictions, including recurring-subscription and cancellation requirements, sales and other taxes, Guildkeeper classification and tax reporting, payment-provider requirements, consumer disclosures, and any licensing obligations.

## 9. Initial jurisdiction scope

The first payment-architecture review for this membership structure is scoped to California, Missouri, Kansas, and New York. That scope does not itself constitute approval to sell memberships in those states or elsewhere. Production activation remains gated on the final reviewed agreements, product behavior, payment configuration, and jurisdiction-specific compliance requirements.
