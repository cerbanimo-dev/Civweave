# Territory Stewardship Agreement — Japan

**Execution draft**  
**Effective date:** August 14, 2026  
**Territory ID:** `jp`  
**Public Steward name:** Taki  
**Home region:** Tokyo, Japan

This Territory Stewardship Agreement (“Agreement”) is between **Cerbanimo LLC** (“Company”) and the individual whose legal identity is completed in the signature block below (“Steward”), professionally/publicly known as **Taki**.

Repository inclusion is not a signature. The Steward’s exact legal name, address and tax/payment identity must be completed and verified before personal payout is enabled.

## 1. Appointment

The Company appoints the Steward to the office of **Territory Steward: Japan**, subject to the August 14, 2026 Territory Stewardship Resolution (“Resolution”). The Steward’s home region is Tokyo.

The `jp` office is the current national Japan office. A later duly appointed, more-specific Japanese territory may supersede this office for Hub Nodes assigned to that territory while leaving `jp` as the parent fallback.

## 2. Stewardship role

The Steward will provide reasonable territorial stewardship appropriate to Civweave’s stage and local needs, including community representation, continuity, local coordination, cultural and language feedback, escalation of Japanese legal/compliance questions, and support for orderly succession.

The office does not grant the Steward general authority to sign contracts, incur debt, open accounts, employ people, make regulated representations, or otherwise bind Cerbanimo LLC or any future Japanese operating entity unless separately authorized in writing.

## 3. Stewardship Share

For qualifying transactions attributed to this Territory while this appointment is in effect, the office earns the **Territory Stewardship Share**, defined as **50% of the existing Cerbanimo cash share** for that transaction lane. The other 50% of that existing Cerbanimo share remains Cerbanimo Global.

This second-stage split is taken **only from the Cerbanimo share**. It does not reduce the compensation of any provider, seller, contributor, system/compute reserve, or **Host Node Steward**.

At current default rates, examples are:

- compute top-up: 2.5% of service net to Territory Stewardship, while the Host Node Steward remains at 25%;
- membership: 12.5% of service net to Territory Stewardship, while the Host Node Steward remains at 25%;
- FellowFare service/learning/tutoring direct charge: 1.25% of the service sale at the default 5% application fee, while the facilitating Host Node Steward remains at 2.5%;
- December 1 annual payout: 2.5% of the annual payout, while the Node Host remains at 10%.

Rates may change only under the economic-governance rules applicable to Civweave. A rate change does not convert this Agreement into equity.

## 4. Office-linked right; not equity

The Stewardship Share is compensation associated with the office and term. It is **not equity** and grants no membership, stock, partnership interest, ownership percentage, voting right, dividend right, liquidation preference, or residual claim in Cerbanimo LLC, Civweave, or any current or future Japanese operating entity.

The Steward may not sell, assign, pledge, transfer, or bequeath the office-linked future Stewardship Share. When the appointment ends, the Steward stops accruing new Territory Stewardship Share for transactions settling after the effective end of the term, subject to final accounting for amounts properly accrued beforehand.

## 5. Accrual and payout gates

The office may accrue a Stewardship Share before payout onboarding is complete. Cash will remain held rather than transferred until all legally required identity, tax, payment-provider/KYC, cross-border payment, and compensation-processing requirements are satisfied.

The runtime may therefore record an appointment as active while the payout status remains `held-pending-onboarding`.

The parties acknowledge that payment may ultimately be made by Cerbanimo LLC, a duly established Japanese operating entity such as a future Civweave Japan G.K., payroll, an approved contractor/business account, or another lawful payment route. This Agreement does not promise that the Steward will receive a direct transfer from a U.S. Stripe platform account.

## 6. Refunds, disputes, and reversals

If an underlying transaction is refunded, disputed, charged back, or otherwise reversed, the related Stewardship Share is reduced proportionally. The Company or lawful paying entity may reverse a related payout or offset the amount against later office-linked payouts to preserve the adopted split.

## 7. Vacancy and succession

If this office becomes vacant, its future Territory Stewardship Share is held in the **Territory Operations Reserve** and does not automatically revert to Cerbanimo Global. A successor becomes eligible for future office-linked amounts upon appointment and satisfaction of applicable payout gates.

The Steward will reasonably cooperate in transferring territory-specific operational information and access that belongs to the Company, any applicable Japanese operating entity, or the Civweave community to an authorized successor.

## 8. Worker classification and Japanese mandatory law

This Agreement does not attempt to manufacture an employee, independent-contractor, freelancer, officer, or other worker classification by label. The actual relationship and applicable law control.

If Japanese law treats the actual working relationship as employment or otherwise requires payroll, withholding, social-insurance, labor-protection, invoice/reporting, or other mandatory treatment, the Company or applicable Japanese operating entity will use the legally required treatment notwithstanding the “Territory Steward” title or revenue-share formula.

The parties will complete any Japanese and U.S. tax forms, treaty documentation, withholding procedures, payment-provider verification, or cross-border reporting reasonably required before payout.

## 9. Taxes and records

The Company will maintain records of gross transaction basis, the existing Cerbanimo share, Cerbanimo Global amount, Territory Stewardship amount, reversals, amounts held, and amounts paid. The Steward will provide tax forms and identity information reasonably required for lawful payment and reporting.

Neither party is relying on this Agreement as a promise of a particular Japanese or U.S. tax outcome.

## 10. Confidentiality, data, and intellectual property

The Steward must protect nonpublic credentials, personal data, security information, and confidential Company/community information received through the office. This Agreement does not transfer ownership of either party’s pre-existing intellectual property. Contributions to Civweave remain governed by the applicable repository, contributor, employment, contractor, or product terms under which they are made.

## 11. Expenses

No expense is reimbursable merely because it relates to the Territory. The Company or applicable Japanese operating entity must approve reimbursable expenses in advance unless an emergency or separate written policy provides otherwise.

## 12. Term and end of appointment

The appointment begins on the Effective Date and continues until resignation, removal, replacement, expiration under a later resolution, or another lawful end of appointment. Ending the office ends future office-linked accrual but does not erase valid amounts accrued before the effective end date, subject to refunds, disputes, offsets, withholding, and mandatory law.

## 13. Governing law and cross-border completion

Mandatory Japanese and U.S. worker, tax, payment, privacy, consumer, sanctions, and other applicable laws govern regardless of contractual labels. The final governing-law, forum, language-priority, and payer clauses should be completed after the Japanese operating structure and the Steward’s verified legal identity are established.
