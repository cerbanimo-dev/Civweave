**Entity:** Cerbanimo LLC (the “Company”), subject to insertion of its exact state of formation and legal entity details before execution.

## Recitals

The Company develops and supports Civweave, an offline-first community coordination platform in which independent Host Node Stewards operate local infrastructure and users may transact through several distinct payment rails.

The Company wishes to establish a Territory Stewardship system that rewards the ongoing stewardship of geographic operating communities without reducing compensation already allocated to users, providers, Host Node Stewards, contributors, or compute/system reserves.

The Company further wishes to make each Territory Stewardship Share an economic right attached to an office and term of stewardship rather than permanent personal equity.

## Resolutions

### 1. Territory Stewardship offices are created

The Company establishes the office of **Territory Steward** for geographic territories recognized by Civweave. Territories may be national, regional, state/provincial, prefectural, metropolitan, municipal, community-defined, or otherwise nested where legally and operationally appropriate.

A Territory Steward is responsible for local stewardship, community representation, operational coordination, escalation of local legal/compliance needs, and continuity of the Civweave territory described in the applicable Stewardship Agreement.

### 2. The existing Cerbanimo share is subdivided, and nothing else is reduced

For every transaction lane in which Civweave presently allocates a cash share to Cerbanimo, that **existing Cerbanimo share** is subdivided as follows:

- **50% to Cerbanimo Global**; and
- **50% to the applicable Territory Stewardship office**.

This is a second-stage split of the existing Cerbanimo share. It does **not** increase the price or platform fee and does **not** reduce any Host Node Steward share, provider/seller share, contributor share, or system/compute reserve.

At current default rates, this produces the following economics after applicable processor-cost conventions for each lane:

- Compute top-ups: 70% system/compute reserve, 25% Host Node Steward, 2.5% Cerbanimo Global, 2.5% Territory Stewardship.
- Memberships: 50% system/compute reserve, 25% Host Node Steward, 12.5% Cerbanimo Global, 12.5% Territory Stewardship.
- FellowFare services/learning/tutoring at the default 5% application fee: 95% provider before the application fee, 2.5% facilitating Host Node Steward, 1.25% Cerbanimo Global, 1.25% Territory Stewardship.
- December 1 annual reserve payout: 85% eligible cotoken contributors, 10% Node Host, 2.5% Cerbanimo Global, 2.5% Territory Stewardship.

Physical/community goods remain seller-direct with no FellowFare percentage. Shared-domain hosting cost-share is unchanged.

### 3. Territory Stewardship is compensation, not equity

A Stewardship Share is **not equity**. Appointment to an office does not make the Steward a member, shareholder, partner, owner, director, or voting holder of Cerbanimo LLC, Civweave, or any territorial entity unless a separate written instrument expressly grants such status.

A Steward has no right to sell, pledge, assign, bequeath, or retain a future claim to the office-linked Stewardship Share after the Steward’s term ends.

### 4. Territory precedence

Each Hub Node may be assigned a canonical Civweave territory. Where territories overlap, the **most specific active appointed territory** governs the Territory Stewardship Share for transactions facilitated by that Hub. If that territory has no active appointee, routing falls back recursively to the nearest active parent-territory appointment.

Buyer location, seller preference, or checkout input may not redirect the Territory Stewardship Share.

### 5. Vacancy and succession

If no active Territory Steward is available, or if the appointed Steward has not completed the required agreement, identity, tax, or payout onboarding, the Territory Stewardship Share is held in a **Territory Operations Reserve**. It does not revert to Cerbanimo Global merely because an office is vacant or onboarding is incomplete.

Upon resignation, removal, expiration, incapacity, or other end of a Steward’s term, the Steward ceases accruing new office-linked entitlements after the effective end of appointment. A successor becomes eligible for future office-linked entitlements upon appointment, subject to payout gates.

### 6. Refunds and disputes

Any refunded, reversed, disputed, charged-back, or otherwise unwound underlying transaction proportionally reduces the related Territory Stewardship entitlement. If the Territory Stewardship Share has already been transferred, Civweave may reverse or offset the corresponding transfer to preserve the adopted economic split.

### 8. Agreement and payout gates

Appointment may exist before cash payout becomes available. No personal payout shall be released until the applicable Steward has completed the Company’s required:

- Stewardship Agreement acceptance;
- legal identity verification;
- tax and information-reporting documentation;
- payment-provider/KYC onboarding; and
- any jurisdiction-specific worker-classification, payroll, withholding, licensing, or compliance review required by law.

Amounts accruing before those gates clear remain held for the applicable office or Territory Operations Reserve.

### 9. Worker classification is not created by title or contract label

The title “Territory Steward” and the revenue-share formula do not by themselves determine whether a Steward is an employee, independent contractor, member, agent, or another legally recognized relationship. The Company shall classify and process compensation according to the actual facts, applicable federal law, the law of the Steward’s work location, and applicable international rules. If law requires payroll treatment, wage protections, withholding, benefits, or other employment treatment, the Company shall use that treatment notwithstanding any contrary label.

The Company authorizes implementation of this resolution in Civweave’s money-edge, ledger, territory registry, refund/reversal system, annual distribution logic, and related documentation, provided the implementation preserves the economic invariants above and remains fail-closed where identity, compliance, or payout requirements are incomplete.
