This Territory Stewardship Agreement (“Agreement”) is between **Cerbanimo LLC** (“Company”) and the individual whose legal identity is completed in the signature block below (“Steward”), professionally/publicly known as **Cami Ryn Stormcaller**.

Repository inclusion is not a signature. The Steward’s exact legal name and the Company’s formation details must be completed before this Agreement is treated as executed.

## 1. Appointment

The Company appoints the Steward to the office of **Territory Steward: United States**, subject to the August 14, 2026 Territory Stewardship Resolution (“Resolution”). The Steward’s home region is New York.

The `us` office is a national fallback office. If a Hub Node is assigned to a more-specific territory that has an active appointed Steward, that more-specific office receives the Territory Stewardship Share for that Hub. Current local examples include Kansas City, Missouri and Los Angeles, California.

## 2. Stewardship role

The Steward will provide reasonable territorial stewardship appropriate to the stage and needs of Civweave, including community representation, continuity, local coordination, escalation of legal/compliance questions, feedback on local operation, and support for succession when the office changes hands.

The office does not grant the Steward general authority to sign contracts, incur debt, open accounts, employ people, make regulated representations, or otherwise bind the Company unless the Company separately authorizes that act in writing.

## 3. Stewardship Share

For qualifying transactions attributed to this Territory while this appointment is in effect, the office earns the **Territory Stewardship Share**, defined as **50% of the existing Cerbanimo cash share** for that transaction lane. The other 50% of that existing Cerbanimo share remains Cerbanimo Global.

This second-stage split is taken **only from the Cerbanimo share**. It does not reduce the compensation of any provider, seller, contributor, system/compute reserve, or **Host Node Steward**.

At current default rates, examples are:

- compute top-up: 2.5% of service net to Territory Stewardship, while the Host Node Steward remains at 25%;
- membership: 12.5% of service net to Territory Stewardship, while the Host Node Steward remains at 25%;
- FellowFare service/learning/tutoring direct charge: 1.25% of the service sale at the default 5% application fee, while the facilitating Host Node Steward remains at 2.5%;
- December 1 annual payout: 2.5% of the annual payout, while the Node Host remains at 10%.

Rates may change only under the economic-governance rules applicable to Civweave. A rate change does not convert this Agreement into equity.

## 4. Office-linked right; not equity

The Stewardship Share is compensation associated with the office and term. It is **not equity** and grants no membership, stock, partnership interest, ownership percentage, voting right, dividend right, liquidation preference, or residual claim in Cerbanimo LLC, Civweave, or any related entity.

The Steward may not sell, assign, pledge, transfer, or bequeath the office-linked future Stewardship Share. When the appointment ends, the Steward stops accruing new Territory Stewardship Share for transactions settling after the effective end of the term, subject to final accounting for amounts properly accrued beforehand.

## 5. Accrual and payout gates

The office may accrue a Stewardship Share before payout onboarding is complete. Cash will remain held rather than transferred until all legally required identity, tax, payment-provider/KYC, and compensation-processing requirements are satisfied.

The runtime may therefore record an appointment as active while the payout status remains `held-pending-onboarding`.

If payout cannot lawfully be made directly to the Steward, the Company may pay through payroll, an approved territorial entity, an approved contractor/business account, or another lawful method without changing the underlying economic formula.

## 6. Refunds, disputes, and reversals

If an underlying transaction is refunded, disputed, charged back, or otherwise reversed, the related Stewardship Share is reduced proportionally. The Company may reverse a related payout or offset the amount against later office-linked payouts to preserve the adopted split.

## 7. Vacancy and succession

If this office becomes vacant, its future Territory Stewardship Share is held in the **Territory Operations Reserve** and does not automatically revert to Cerbanimo Global. A successor becomes eligible for future office-linked amounts upon appointment and satisfaction of applicable payout gates.

The Steward will reasonably cooperate in transferring territory-specific operational information and access that belongs to the Company or Civweave community to an authorized successor.

## 8. Worker and owner classification

This Agreement does not attempt to manufacture a tax or employment classification by label. The actual legal relationship controls.

Because the Steward may also be an owner/member/manager of Cerbanimo LLC, payments must be characterized according to the Company’s actual federal, state, and local tax classification at the time of payment. Depending on entity elections and facts, the lawful treatment may be owner draw/distribution, payroll compensation, another owner-payment category, or another required form. The title “Territory Steward” does not itself make a payment wages.

If applicable law requires employee treatment, payroll, withholding, minimum-wage/overtime protections, benefits, workers’ compensation, unemployment coverage, or other employment treatment, the Company will use the required treatment notwithstanding a contrary label.

## 9. Taxes and records

The Company will maintain records of gross transaction basis, the existing Cerbanimo share, Cerbanimo Global amount, Territory Stewardship amount, reversals, amounts held, and amounts paid. The Steward will provide tax forms and identity information reasonably required for lawful payment and reporting.

Neither party is relying on this Agreement as a promise of a particular tax outcome.

## 10. Confidentiality, data, and intellectual property

The Steward must protect nonpublic credentials, personal data, security information, and confidential Company/community information received through the office. This Agreement does not transfer ownership of either party’s pre-existing intellectual property. Contributions to Civweave remain governed by the applicable repository, contributor, employment, contractor, or product terms under which they are made.

## 11. Expenses

No expense is reimbursable merely because it relates to the Territory. The Company must approve reimbursable expenses in advance unless an emergency or separate written policy provides otherwise.

## 12. Term and end of appointment

The appointment begins on the Effective Date and continues until resignation, removal, replacement, expiration under a later resolution, or another lawful end of appointment. Ending the office ends future office-linked accrual but does not erase valid amounts accrued before the effective end date, subject to refunds, disputes, offsets, and legal withholding.
