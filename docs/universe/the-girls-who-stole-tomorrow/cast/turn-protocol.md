# Author-review, cast-guided dialogue rounds

## Ownership
Use this folder as **input** to the canonical Performance Studio and selected-model inference owner, never as a second chat system or automatic agent runner. The Loom Coordinates describe the story world and rule certainty; they do not prove a live simulation ran.

The cast file gives distinct character instructions. Before each draft turn, supply only: (1) the current approved world rules and scene-local Loom Coordinates; (2) this character's sheet and observed knowledge; (3) the previous **spoken** lines; and (4) a precise task. No character receives another character's private thoughts, hidden spoiler fields, or the full manuscript automatically. Keep private author notes out of hosted endpoints without explicit consent.

## Proposed turn order
1. **Loom**: issue a 100–250 word local scene packet, distinguishing hard constraints, direct observations, and unknowns. Never fabricate world actions or verified receipts.
2. **Landry**: propose the scene's first-person sensory perceptions and one question; cannot reveal what the other Landry intends.
3. **Delia**: challenge an assumption and propose a *consensual* intervention. Her own independent perspective is expressed only through Landry-observable speech and shared experience.
4. **Maggie**: draft a punchy spoken line, challenge melodrama and detect coercive wording. Keep Magnus independently named.
5. **Magnus/Merlin**: Magnus proposes a test; Merlin supplies a separate systems/boundaries review. Their temporary creative pairing never silently fuses identities.
6. **Gaia/Kami**: test the scene against hope, autonomous choice, noncoercion and consequences. One unified editorial role for this project; not a real-world supernatural fact.
7. **Supporting actor**, if present: speak **only** to what that actor could know. A role sheet cannot create unseen memories.
8. **Loom**: update observable local facts and open questions, without upgrading proposed dialogue into approved canon.
9. **Human editor**: selects, revises or rejects lines; only approved text moves into manuscript canon. A scene may receive multiple rounds.

## Five overlays
Apply the existing character-fleet benchmark overlays in this order: canon/voice; relationships and knowledge continuity; specific situated action; principled dissent/uncertainty; integrated self-check. They are authoring constraints, not automatic model invocations.

## Hard gates
- Never let a fictional pilot gain Guild/Passport/voting/purchasing/publishing authority.
- User-author review is necessary for any role assignment, plot reveal, published chapter or adaptation.
- No hidden scene knowledge; preserve distinct Landry/Delia and Magnus/Maggie identities.
- Chapter one uses Landry's POV, so the dialogue must come from her hearing, memory or explicitly shared interaction.
- No reserved final-scene profanity; avoid mechanical opposition phrasing and unearned exposition.
- Only factual story observations update Loom Coordinates. Improv outcomes are provisional pending author acceptance.
