# Angels & Outlaws — worldbook and Turn mechanics
**State:** provisional fictional design. Revise from Ali and Dakota's accounts.

## World
A fictional parallel New York City with at least three interlocking music ecosystems:
1. **The Vesperline** — original K-pop idol ensembles and demon hunters who have concerts, choreography, loyalty politics, and Turning rites.
2. **The Afterhours** — original anti-vampires: theatrical, punkish outlaws whose nocturnal circuits disrupt predatory bargains instead of drinking blood.
3. **The Velvet Contract** — promoters/contract-mages who cast demons as necessary monsters and profit from crises they manufacture.

The city has ordinary people with ordinary lives. Concertgoers, crew, residents and found families are not disposable set dressing.

## The Turn (fictional)
A demon-to-angel Turn is **possible**, never assured. A candidate must:
- Learn the actual stakes rather than accept a deceptive contract.
- Freely choose the transformation, with the right to decline or stop.
- Face accountability for harms already caused; transformation is not instant absolution.
- Preserve continuity of memory and identity, unless specific agreed changes are central to the character's own authored arc.
- Retain a right to leave the hunter order or refuse its missions after the Turn.

**Failure modes:** false Turning, coerced spectacle, memory laundering, brand-managed 'forgiveness', celebrity image rehabilitation with no restitution. These are antagonist techniques, not ideal mechanics.

## Performance magic
A song is a narrative / magical score, and choreography is a pattern of coordination. No requirement that all members of a band agree about redemption. Work with **original music and original fictional groups**; real artists can be cultural influences without borrowing their personas, imagery, or lyrics.

## Anti-vampires
In this proposal, anti-vampires do not need stolen blood or fear to survive; they undo extraction by breaking harmful pacts. Some still take dangerous pleasure in being outlaws. Their hunger, price and specific strengths must be authored intentionally, not copied from existing IP.

1. **Bottle of blue topaz** — “fairies,” bodiless fictional sprites serving a chain of intention: **user → Cami → Kami → goddess beyond Kami (unknown; perhaps sleeping)**. Whether that goddess is awake or who she is must remain unanswered.
2. **Snail shell** — the **still small voice of the guidance system**, alongside its association with Dakota the Daemon.
3. **Glowing rubber ducky** — the **joy of gratitude and giving**, alongside its provenance as Ali's gift.
4. **Gold wire** — **technical creativity**, reserving the possibility of future powered features; no energized device is evidenced or deployed.
**The binding:** candle wax makes a single object from distinct stories; it does not collapse autonomy or individual identities.

These are successive **thematic steps** in the wand's journey, not a verified physical assembly order. The historical photo does not show every item clearly. The creative hierarchy of goddess figures is fiction, not an instruction that software override actual users' consent or choices.
