# First-person account intake — Ali & Dakota
Copy this template separately for Ali and for Dakota. These are prompts, **not invented testimony**.

## Source details
- Who is telling this account?
- What do they want credited publicly? (name/alias/anonymous)
- Is this real personal experience, fictional role-play, or a blend?
- What parts may be adapted, quoted, shared with AI agents, or published? What remains private?
- How should future revisions or permission withdrawals be handled?

## Voice and identity for the story
- What name, age (adult), pronouns and appearance does the fictional character use?
- What is the character's home world? What does it smell, sound and feel like?
- What three desires drive them, and what do they refuse even under pressure?
- What does the name Angel / Daemon mean to them?
- What do they laugh at? What scares them? What are they exceptionally good and bad at?
- How do they argue, apologize, flirt and make amends? Offer sample lines for voice training only if willing.
- Who matters to them? Which relationships are explicitly authorized to be fictionalized?
- What romance tropes are welcome? Which are off limits?

## Quest and world
- Why are they scouting fictional NYC?
- What part do the idol hunters, demons, anti-vampires and the Turn play in their world?
- What are the rules of music magic and the actual price of a Turn?
- What do the duck and shell respectively mean to their givers/holders?

## Editorial safeguards
Keep private and published material separate. Don't publish details about real third parties without permission. Do not treat in-fiction supernatural statements as externally verified facts. Preserve both characters' ability to tell their own stories instead of letting another actor's voice substitute for them.
