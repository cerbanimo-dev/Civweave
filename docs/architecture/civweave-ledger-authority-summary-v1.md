# Civweave ledger authority summary v1

- Validation Ledger proves claims and fulfillment.
- Reward Ledger owns Skill XP, Acorns, and Buttons. Acorns and Buttons are earn/burn-only and never transferable.
- Contribution Ledger owns Cotokens and endeavor contribution shares.
- Fulfillment Ledger links a request, fulfiller, proof result, requester burn, and fulfiller reward without transferring the burned asset.
- Guilds are scoped replicas/routing services; personal devices retain Passport authority.
- Passport, Living School skill totals, FellowFare wallet totals, and Chronicle are rebuildable projections.
