# The Path Returns
Status: PROPOSED REWRITE — pending Cami Ryn Tharp approval
Original fragment: "Willow branches weaving dances / Living life and taking chances / Prancing past a shadow's cast / Let's make, some memories last" and later copperhead/turtle/path lines. Missing middle verse recreated as fresh draft by Maggie (fictional co-writer scene).

## Verse 1
Willow branches weaving dances
Living life and taking chances
Prancing past a shadow's cast
Let's make some memories last

Barefoot running through the clover
Laughing as the clouds roll over
Every winding trail we roam
Finds another way back home

## Verse 2
Foxglove shivers in the clearing
Something moves beyond our hearing
Little birds abandon song
Something hungry comes along

Silver teeth among the flowers
Thunderheads and summer showers
Every creature knows the game
Nothing here will stay the same

## Chorus
Oh, the willow bends and sways
Through the nights and through the days
Round the river, round the bend
Every ending turns again

Oh, the wind knows where we've been
Whispers through the leaves again
Take my hand and count to ten
Come on, let's get lost again

## Verse 3
Copperhead comes close to heel
Closer call with flesh and steel
A turtle finds a sunny bath
A footstep finds a moonlit path

The path returns from self
Through the woods and somewhere else
Hear the branches clap and sing
Wonder what tomorrow brings

## Final Chorus
Oh, the willow bends and sways
Through the nights and through the days
Round the river, round the bend
Every ending turns again

Oh, the wind knows where we've been
Whispers through the leaves again
Take my hand and count to ten
Come on, let's get lost again
