# RC21.2 · Actionable Quad

Commonweave's central AI is now a bounded cross-system operator rather than a detached chat surface.

## Added

- Persistent Commonweave conversation thread across navigation and application handoffs.
- Live ecosystem search with record-aware open and explanation actions.
- Journey creation from real Cerbanimo, Living School, FellowFare, and Anarchadia records.
- Obligation-first guidance that surfaces urgent and review-stage work before suggesting new work.
- Structured handoff staging for projects, curricula, exchanges, and governance proposals.
- Human review queue with explicit approve or reject decisions.
- Approved handoffs are queued in destination-specific local inboxes and never mutate canonical data silently.
- CSS/SVG glowing route overlay with reduced-motion support.
- Offline caching for the actionable Quad runtime.

## Safety boundary

The Quad may search, explain, navigate, create journeys, and prepare actions. Cross-system writes remain review-required and are delivered to destination-specific inboxes for application-side consumption.
