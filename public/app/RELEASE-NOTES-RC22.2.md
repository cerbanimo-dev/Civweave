# Civweave RC22.2 · Negotiated Commitments and Predictive Portfolio Stewardship

- Added a reversible scenario simulator for prioritizing, pausing, delaying, and delegating intentions.
- Added before-and-after portfolio forecasts for deadline risk, collisions, labor, energy, and released capacity.
- Added a commitment ledger for people, tools, spaces, vehicles, and resources with tentative, requested, accepted, partial, confirmed, declined, released, and expired states.
- Added portfolio capacity records and budget pressure across money, time, labor, energy, and community credits.
- Added predictive signals for expiring commitments, unconfirmed dependencies, and resource exhaustion.
- Added an adaptive seven-day agenda weighted by priority, risk, and remaining work.
- Added the Scenario Forge world screen and bounded review handoff for scenario adoption.
- Retained destination ownership, review gates, local-first persistence, and offline operation.
