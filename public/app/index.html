FIELD NOTES FROM ANARCHADIA

While the local world assembles…

Civweave’s civic realm begins after a collapse, when children recover old networks and decide that rebuilding society is something people can learn by doing.

YEAR 0 · THE ARRIVAL

The old systems are quiet.

After the Collapse, young arrivals find the remains of pre-disaster networks. The first question is not how to restore the old world exactly as it was. It is what should be worth carrying forward.

Learning begins with survival, memory, and the right to understand the systems around you.

YEAR 20 · THE SETTLEMENT

A camp becomes a community.

People learn cooperation, tool-making, shelter, exchange, and basic governance together. Civic life is not a chapter in a textbook. It is the practical craft of deciding how to live with other people.

The learner’s path grows from Self, to Tools, to Community.

YEAR 50 · THE NETWORK

The settlements find one another.

The network expands across cities and territories. Education becomes part of civilization’s infrastructure, carrying practical knowledge between communities without requiring every community to surrender its autonomy.

Local first does not mean alone. Federation is built from capable places choosing to connect.

YEAR 100 · THE FEDERATION

A civilization learns to steward itself.

A planetary federation emerges from communities that can make, repair, govern, teach, and regenerate. The ambition is not a frictionless utopia. It is a society with enough civic competence to notice problems and do something about them.

The path eventually reaches Planet and Stars, but it never stops being grounded in the people nearby.

THE COMMONS

Nine domains, one shared world.

Shelter, inner development, manufacturing, economy, governance, energy, navigation, water, and health form the Commons. People move through simulation, real-world practice, and reflection until knowledge becomes something they can use for one another.

Arrival → Explorer → Builder → Citizen → Founder → Builder+ → Steward → Elder.
