If confusion is new or severe

Sudden confusion, fainting, difficulty speaking, weakness, or feeling physically unsafe deserves urgent medical attention. Call local emergency services or ask someone nearby for help. If confusion keeps happening, a clinician can help look for causes such as sleep, medications, stress, or a health issue.
