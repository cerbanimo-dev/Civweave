1 · Guardian recovery

Your wallet key is separate from the transport-device key. Recovery encrypts the wallet key and splits the recovery key among guardians. Use distinct people/devices and move each share somewhere independent before confirming.

Do not send recovery shares through ordinary public/federated mesh objects.

2 · Federation policy anchor

A value-bearing federation needs at least three independent genesis roots and a threshold-signed policy anchor. Paste an anchor draft or signed anchor below. Genesis operators can add their local signature, then pass the JSON to the next signer.

3 · Validator enrollment

Validator membership is wallet-root based, device-bound, recovery-gated, and expanded through attestations from already eligible roots.

4 · Emergency wallet containment

A freeze request is only a proposal. Selected validator roots must witness it and reach committee quorum before a wallet is blocked from gaining new secure transfer finality.
