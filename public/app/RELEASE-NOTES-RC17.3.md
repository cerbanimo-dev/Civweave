# Civweave 1.0.0 RC17.3 · Anarchadia Civic Operations

RC17.3 audits the supplied Anarchadia room art for implied capabilities and promotes the most useful missing stations into durable local-first workflows. The illustrated hall remains the primary navigation layer, while the original Charter Forge remains available as the precision Workbench.

## Proposal Commons

- Petition support and concern signals are now preserved as portable operational records.
- Support signals explicitly do not certify identity, consent, quorum, legitimacy, or ratification.
- Proposals now carry discussion notes, contribution types, visible counts, and workflow stages.
- Visible stages can progress from proposed through petitioning, deliberating, voting, and outcome recording without creating an automatic outcome.

## Hub Commons

- Bounded workgroups can be created with purpose, scope, facilitator, status, and next action.
- Commons bulletins can be posted with category and optional expiry.
- Neither workgroups nor bulletins acquire undeclared authority or infer participation.

## Federation Chamber

- Hubs can draft bounded federation messages linked to explicit bridge contracts.
- Hub-scoped adoption signals record proposed, reviewed, adopted-in-scope, declined, reconsidering, or superseded responses.
- Messages and adoption signals remain local declarations unless separate signed transport or governance records exist.

## The Forge

- Simulations and tests now have durable experiment records with hypotheses, methods, success criteria, status, results, and residual uncertainty.
- Experiments produce evidence but never deploy or ratify a proposal automatically.

## Amendment Ledger

- Charter snapshots can now be used to draft append-only rollback proposals.
- Rollback does not erase current or superseded records, dissent, downstream effects, or distributed copies.
- Every rollback enters Proposal Commons for ordinary review and declared outcome handling.

## Observatory

- An actionable alert center derives privacy-bounded reminders from blocking threats, incomplete rights duties, serious bugs, invalid bridge contracts, and stale proposals.
- Dismissing an alert removes only the local reminder. It never edits the underlying record.

## Portability

The new civic records participate in selective export and conflict-preserving import:

- decision bundles include petition signals, discussions, bulletins, and Forge experiments
- role bundles include workgroups
- bridge bundles include federation messages and adoption signals
- amendment bundles include rollback proposals

## Canonical Anarchadia brand

The current Anarchadia logo is now used consistently by:

- Civweave service cards and room headers
- the standalone Anarchadia Workbench sidebar
- the Rook advisory surface
- standalone PWA icons and Apple touch icon
- maskable launcher assets
- Pocket Campus
- service-worker offline caches

The legacy geometric placeholder icon and plain-letter sidebar badge have been removed.

## Preserved boundaries

RC17.3 keeps every prior governance boundary intact: no machine-certified legitimacy, no inferred consent, no automatic adoption, no automatic deployment, dissent preservation, human approval gates, selective exchange, default-off bridges, local-first persistence, and replaceable operation.
