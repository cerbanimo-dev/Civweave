# Commonweave RC22.1 — Grounded Resource Negotiation and Portfolio Intelligence

## Added

- Portfolio-wide health, collision, overload, and shared-dependency analysis.
- Ranked gap candidates using suitability, availability, trust, distance, cost, and accessibility signals.
- Review-gated collaborator consent and availability requests.
- Local resource reservations, competing-claim detection, and release controls.
- Cross-intention schedule collision and collaborator overload detection.
- Reviewed recommendations to pause, sequence, delegate, or coordinate intentions.
- Natural-language portfolio priority guidance through the Quad.
- Daily or weekly material-change executive portfolio briefs.
- Portfolio dashboard with a live intention map, decisions, claims, and consent requests.

## Safety boundaries

Commonweave does not silently assign people, reserve canonical FellowFare inventory, reorder plans, pause intentions, or amend destination records. These operations remain local proposals or review-gated actions until a destination confirms them.
