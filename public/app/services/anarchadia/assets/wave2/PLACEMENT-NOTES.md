# Anarchadia — six-screen plan and asset placement

Target scene canvas: **941 × 2072 px**, vertical. Build the architectural backgrounds without Merlin, logos, props, icons, interface copy, or active holograms; add those as separate live layers using this kit.

## 1. Commons Hall

Purpose: orient the user around active plans, decisions, unresolved objections, and next actions.

- Reserve the upper 18% for the transparent Anarchadia logo and realm/state controls.
- Place `merlin/01-welcome-guide.png` at lower right, about 28–34% of canvas width.
- Center the blank zine bulletin board in the middle third; populate it with live plan cards.
- Use active-plan, proposal, objection, and execution icons as status markers—not architectural ornament.

## 2. Merlin’s Control Room

Purpose: conversation, delegation, agent selection, schedules, triggers, and automation supervision.

- Leave a character bay in the lower-left quarter for `merlin/02-conversation-listening.png`.
- Place the automation control bench across the lower-middle foreground.
- Keep the upper-middle clear for live chat, agent chips, and a workflow visualization.
- Use delegation token, trigger sensor, schedule clock, condition switch, approval gate, and audit ledger as interactive layers.

## 3. Proposal Forge

Purpose: create and edit plans, proposals, bug reports, and feature requests.

- Place `merlin/03-proposal-forge.png` at lower right.
- Place the drafting table in the middle/lower third; its paper remains a live editor surface.
- Hang the amendment placard to the left of the editor.
- Use proposal, edit, bug, and feature icons in the creation toolbar.

## 4. Assembly Floor

Purpose: discussion, amendments, evidence review, and conflict mapping.

- Center the circular debate table in the lower-middle with clear participant slots.
- Place `merlin/04-conflict-map.png` above or just behind the table, never embedded into the wall.
- Put the evidence projector to one side; its blank frame holds citations and source previews.
- Keep the upper half open for the thread, amendment stack, and conflict map.

## 5. Consent Chamber

Purpose: consent checks, voting, objections, appeals, and recorded decisions.

- Place the consent console at lower center and `merlin/05-consent-decision.png` to its right.
- Put the objection flag rack on the left edge and the appeal podium deeper in the scene.
- Reserve a large clean central region for options, thresholds, objections, and decision rationale.
- Use consent and objection/appeal emblems only as live state indicators.

## 6. Federation Engine

Purpose: execute accepted decisions, assign work, automate follow-through, and coordinate other realms/nodes.

- Center the empty federation node-map frame in the upper-middle and populate it dynamically.
- Place `merlin/06-automation-execution.png` in the lower-left quarter.
- Use the execution dispatch board and automation control bench as foreground interaction anchors.
- Add workflow chain, task assignment, dependency map, handoff portal, audit ledger, and success/rollback artifacts as live controls.

## Shared visual rules

- Architecture: riveted blackened steel, patched masonry, exposed pipes/cables, reclaimed wood, cyan instrument glass, yellow hazard paint, hot-pink brush markings.
- Preserve negative space for UI. Blank parchment and glass in these props are deliberate content surfaces.
- Use cyan for information, yellow for attention, hot pink for agency/change, white for readable copy, and charcoal for structure.
- Do not bake Merlin, emblems, icons, cards, flags, holograms, or readable text into a background.
- The logo belongs near entrances and global navigation; the anarchy emblem may recur sparingly as stamped metal or paint, but avoid wallpapering it.

## Generation record

Mode: new image generation guided by the supplied exterior, logo, and original Merlin references. Four prompt families were used: six-pose Merlin redesign; governance lifecycle emblems; automation/delegation artifacts; and blank interactive interior props. All sheets were generated on removable chroma green and exported as transparent PNG layers.
