# Synthetically Ratified Provisional Constitution

> “Ratified” here means only that the six synthetic delegates met the convention's provisional voting rule. It is not community ratification and authorizes no deployment, implementation, or real-person governance.

## Article 01 — Purpose Identity And Non Goals

## Exact revised motion

The blockquoted text below is the complete motion placed before the delegates:

> **1. Purpose and identity.** Anarchadia begins as a provisional, optional, local-first constitutional toolkit for groups that govern themselves, some of which may later choose to federate. The first product hypothesis is one named candidate community completing one bounded charter lifecycle, not a claim that all communities need software. Its anarcho-syndicalist origin is project provenance and a steward commitment to voluntary association, member self-government, solidarity, bounded and recallable power, dissent, export, exit, and fork. No person or community must affirm that identity, accept a preset constitution, or adopt every offered primitive to use, amend, refuse, or leave the tool.
>
> **2. Bounded journey and legitimacy.** The first real-community pilot is limited to deciding whether and how to draft or import, inspect, deliberate, adopt or reject, amend, preserve dissent about, and export a charter and minimal decision record. The record may state the operative versions, initiating and deciding roles, affected classes, declared procedure and threshold, declared outcome, amendment or reconsideration condition, dissenter-chosen disclosure, provenance, and export or exit terms. It need not collect full transcripts, attendance, civil identities, device identifiers, social graphs, private drafts, or motivational profiles. Anarchadia may record what a group declared; it may not infer or certify authority, legitimacy, consensus, rights compliance, or ratification from fields, tallies, signatures, cryptography, or software state.
>
> **3. Agency and material non-coercion.** Digital use, political affirmation, civil-identity disclosure, technical competence, possession of a private device or secret, and quantified contribution may not be conditions of necessities, membership standing, political voice, timely influence, or access to authoritative civic records. Care, subsistence, disability, accessibility, domestic, emotional, and ecological work may not be compelled, scored, ranked, compared, or profiled as a condition of those protections. Software and administrators may not direct production or reduce, redirect, ration, or condition core survival resources. Any later capability with that effect is rights-critical, outside initial scope, and referred to affected humans; referral does not authorize it. A participant-chosen, risk-assessed non-digital or assisted path must provide the same in-scope civic effects without requiring identical or equally exposed records, a compelled or recurring unpaid proxy, or a transcriber empowered to alter or legitimate an offline act.
>
> **4. Interface and institutional power.** The toolkit must not use behavioral or motivational profiling, engagement optimization, covert personalization, ideological classification, interim popularity signals, or conformity cues that punish dissent, nonparticipation, or exit. User-invoked, session-local accessibility choices are permitted only when disclosed, resettable, non-inferential, and not reused for political steering. Operative language, controlling text, defaults, summaries, roles, and powers must be visible, community-revisable, and open to dissent. Founder intent is provenance, not veto. No founder, template author, maintainer, host, administrator, delegate, or service may be irreplaceable; silently select binding defaults; force an upgrade; block authorized export, refusal, exit, or fork; erase or conclusively resolve dissent or a disputed record; suspend underlying standing; or hold permanent, opaque, unbounded, or unrecallable authority.
>
> **5. Local-first and contested records.** Local-first is a continuity and data-sovereignty boundary, not a security, privacy, consent, or legitimacy guarantee. On a supported local installation, authorized participants must be able to inspect and edit a charter; record proposals, declared outcomes, amendments, and voluntarily preserved dissent; create protected, selectively scoped exports; and restore authorized community records while public Internet, external DNS, remote authentication, and every irreplaceable remote service are unavailable. The record must remain readable after restart and restorable on replaceable infrastructure; manual file exchange is sufficient for the first pilot. No party may hold the sole usable authoritative copy. Transport, timestamps, import order, connectivity, or administrator preference may not erase or legitimate conflicting records; their versions, provenance, and dispute status remain for human reconciliation.
>
> **6. Privacy, recovery, and ecosystem boundary.** Article 01 authorizes no required civil-identity registry or proof, default persistent identifier across groups or sibling systems, key escrow, privileged decryption, administrator impersonation or credential reset, identity-revealing recovery, silent alteration of dissent, or default transfer of personal identifiers, private history, motivational data, recovery data, or cross-system linkage. "Recovery" here means restoration of authorized community records, not identity, credential, key, or account recovery. The first pilot requires no live connection to Cerbanimo or AUREA: Anarchadia holds constitutional artifacts and declared authority; Cerbanimo remains the sibling system for work, needs, resources, proof, skills, projects, and completion; AUREA remains the sibling system for reflection, attention, and intrinsic motivation. Any later bridge must separately state its purpose, fields, recipients, retention, deletion and withdrawal limits, re-identification and onward-copy risks, authorization, export and exit, amendment, jurisdictional review, and affected-human ratification.
>
> **7. Pilot and no-build gate.** Before community-specific implementation or a live pilot, the candidate community must define its actual bottleneck, compare at least one existing-tool or non-software alternative, and retain a no-build choice. Before authoritative records, the pilot must publish a function-level labor-and-power register and deployment envelope; identify replaceable roles and funded or agreed inputs; and, with synthetic data and non-specialist participants representing actual access conditions, demonstrate the offline charter journey, scoped export, clean restore, safe non-digital record verification and contest, consequence comprehension, and administrator replacement. Before real sensitive data, it must document adversaries, data classes, access, retention, export, restore, deletion limits, device-loss behavior, and incident duties, and test actual shared-device, stress, disability, language, and connectivity conditions. A safety, standing, comprehension, continuity, transcription, labor, or replacement failure narrows or stops the pilot rather than expanding scope or shifting risk to participants.
>
> **8. Non-goals and deferred mechanisms.** Anarchadia is not a source of social legitimacy, currency replacement, global planning authority, persuasion or propaganda system, universal identity layer, or survival-resource allocator. Its first release is not an automated federation, cross-community authority, mandatory sibling bridge, currency or labor-credit system, contribution scorer, resource allocator, identity-recovery system, custom mesh, hypervisor, bare-metal operating system, or automatic reconciliation service. Formal voting beyond recording a community's declared procedure, delegation and recall, federation, identity and credentials, pseudonymous continuity, account or key recovery, dissent authentication, synchronization and conflict resolution, resource allocation, emergency security power, custom networking, and every sibling bridge require separate evidence, specification, amendment, primary-jurisdiction review and co-signatures, adversarial and accessibility testing, affected-human ratification, and preserved refusal, dissent, recall where power is delegated, export, exit, and fork.
>
> **9. Human authority.** A candidate community may accept, revise, or reject this identity, intervention, burden, and risk and may choose no software. Binding use requires accessible, voluntary, safely facilitated, non-extractive affected-community review with authority to amend or reject and with dissent preserved. Synthetic approval establishes only a provisional project boundary; it does not select a constituency, authorize deployment, or equal community ratification. Before Article 02 proceeds, a human reviewer must create the docket-required `HUMAN_APPROVAL.md` recording the reviewing people or community, scope, procedure, authorization, dissent, conditions, and date. That artifact records a procedural gate only and does not certify contested community legitimacy.

## Article 02 — Human Agency And Voluntary Association

## Exact revised motion

> **Participation, affiliation, disclosure, and software use shall be voluntary.** No person shall lose necessities, standing, voice, operative records, earned wages, vested claims, correction, appeal, export, or exit because they refuse, defer, dissent, withdraw, use an assisted or non-digital path, or decline an ideology.
>
> Before a rights-affecting act, the affected person shall receive through a chosen accessible path the exact act, scope, affected people, rule, duration, material consequences and maximum cost, data use, retention, correction, contest, export, exit, and every duty or irreducible copy that may survive withdrawal. Accept, defer, refuse, and request assistance shall receive comparable weight, without preselection, countdown, repetition, popularity cues, loss framing, rewards, penalties, profiling, or retained comprehension narratives. Any comprehension check is participant-chosen, non-punitive, session-local, and never certification of inner voluntariness. Consent expires on material change.
>
> Refusal and withdrawal may be lodged through any ratified equal-effect digital, assisted, or offline path without public Internet, external DNS, remote authentication, a sole service, or a sole administrator. Receipt stops new reliance within the receiving body's control. Outage, delay, failed delivery, silence, stale replication, technical inability, disputed duty, or a comprehension result never creates or renews consent. Contrary records remain contested with minimized provenance for human review; no timestamp, signature, credential, connectivity advantage, or administrator decides legitimacy.
>
> Only return of identified community property, a capped documented pre-withdrawal expense, or a bounded transfer task necessary to prevent immediate harm may survive, and only if separately accepted beforehand. Personal labor performance, future dues, contribution targets, penalties, interest, compounding, transfer, collateral claims on necessities, forfeiture of wages or vested claims, and retrospective exit charges shall not survive. A claimant or its software shall not decide its own contested claim or delay independent rights.
>
> Any receipt is community-and-act scoped, least-linkable, unavailable for profiling or unrelated reuse, replaceably verifiable, and limited to a random act reference, receiving role, request type, receipt state, necessary qualified sequence evidence, rule version, and contest status. It omits civil/device identity, location, reason, comprehension content, safety contacts, readable association, and stable cross-context history. Irreducible copies, deletion limits, and valid contested duties are disclosed honestly.
>
> Before real-person use, affected humans shall define success and complete an adversarial exercise covering accessible explanation, unpressured refusal, shared-device coercion, offline withdrawal, delayed and inaccurate transcription, contrary connected acts, service and administrator replacement, duty contest, necessity continuity, correction, selective export, remedy, and safe exit. Loss of equal effect, privacy, material continuity, contestability, or timely withdrawal blocks digital mediation; redesign, manual practice, deferral, or no build remains valid.

Failure requires continued deferral or no identity subsystem.

Before implementation, affected people and non-specialists shall exercise inaccessible notice, private assistance, offline filing, safe delay, interim continuity, independent review, selective export, correction, automatic expiry, reviewer/service replacement, restoration, and conflicting-record reconciliation. Impersonation, unauthorized decryption, avoidable exposure, unpaid proxy dependence, lost filing, ceremonial remedy, or unstaffed queue blocks mediation.

Any new field, purpose, audience, authority, retention, shared service, or linkage requires a fresh map, adversarial review, and affected-person decision.
