# FellowFare Mall Screen and Asset Plan

FellowFare 0.4.0 establishes a complete navigable mall before final art is available. Every room has a stable scene ID, portal graph, classic-interface handoffs, action mapping, placeholder environment, directory entry, and offline-safe persistence. Final backgrounds can replace placeholders without changing application logic.

## Spatial structure

- Main Atrium: universal arrival, directory, featured activity, and all-wing access.
- East Wing: Marketplace, Free Store, Skill Shop, and Tool Rental.
- West Wing: Help Desk, Resource Center, Volunteer Hub, and Community Pantry.
- North Wing: Community Workshop, Repair Café, and Makers Market.
- South Wing: Shipping Center, Ride Share, and Regional Transit.
- Upper Gallery: Housing Exchange, Time Bank, Community Organizations, and Event Center.
- Rooftop Commons: garden, solar canopy, outdoor learning, farmers market, and events.
- Service Level: Loading Dock, Warehouse, Maintenance Hall, Lost & Found, Safety & Consent Office, and Recycling Center.

## Functional state now

- 31 navigable scenes with reciprocal portals.
- Directory kiosk searches labels, functions, floors, and wings.
- Visual counters open existing marketplace, assemblies, inbox, profile, Loom, and composer flows.
- Category-specific storefronts pre-filter the classic market.
- Current need, offer, collective, and arrangement counts appear in every room.
- Last room persists locally.
- CSS environments serve as automatic fallback until scene artwork exists.
- Mall is the default FellowFare route; Classic Market remains one tap away.
