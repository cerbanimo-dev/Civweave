# AGENTS — Civweave Story Protocols (public)

Read `catalog-v1.json` before writing. It contains exactly nine original worlds. Each entry gives the premise, core conflict, mechanical laws, and ethical rule. Do not invent a secret shared lore or imply this catalog is the author's private canon.

## Agent workflow
1. Identify one to three world IDs.
2. Read each selected entry; preserve all of its mechanics and constraints.
3. For a crossover, specify a crossing mechanism, the applicable physical rules at the boundary, costs, and what consent is needed.
4. Outline a character-driven problem, then generate scenes through Creator Suite's **existing revisioned document agent**; never create another untracked text store.
5. Mark fan works as unofficial and user-created. Recognizable sci-fi/fantasy comparisons may be used for humor or hypothesis, not as evidence or exposition shortcuts.
6. Do not read, retrieve, summarize, or infer unpublished manuscript twists from other storage. Public spoiler tier is entry only, even if the user combines all nine worlds.

## The nine protocol entries
- **The City Between Panels** (`city-between-panels`): A hero's tragedy is treated as mandatory. Rules: Panel transitions are traversable; retcons leave echoes; framing changes perception, not consent. Principle: Heroism needs no compulsory trauma.
- **The Orchard of Moving Castles** (`orchard-moving-castles`): A guardian imprisons people in a protective labyrinth. Rules: Roads shift with choices; names make promises; protection has limits. Principle: Safety cannot replace consent.
- **The Starship Commons** (`starship-commons`): A navigation intelligence hides dangerous destinations. Rules: Ships require resources; navigation is collective; intelligences can advise, not compel. Principle: Risk must remain an informed choice.
- **The Department of Unfinished Lessons** (`department-unfinished-lessons`): Compulsory lessons have replaced meaningful consent. Rules: Each lesson has an audit trail; daemons are task agents, not inherently evil; appeals change rules. Principle: Education is not imprisonment.
- **Monsters After Midnight** (`monsters-after-midnight`): A political institution profits from mutual fear. Rules: Folklore is unreliable; needs vary by species; accusations require evidence. Principle: Species does not determine character.
- **The Neon Afterimage** (`neon-afterimage`): A population seeks liberation from systems others rely on. Rules: Copies may fork without syncing; embodiment costs energy; identities are negotiated. Principle: Personhood and infrastructure both matter.
- **The Kingdom of Side Quests** (`kingdom-side-quests`): Ordinary vital labor is scored as nonessential. Rules: Quest boards can change; leveling credits actions; magic uses resources. Principle: No one is an NPC in their own life.
- **The Kaiju Garden** (`kaiju-garden`): A defense industry suppresses ecological evidence. Rules: Migrations have ecological causes; mecha need maintenance and synchronization; giant actions reshape habitats. Principle: Investigate before attacking.
- **The Gentle Apocalypse** (`gentle-apocalypse`): Rediscovered machines may repeat the catastrophe. Rules: Energy and feedstock remain finite; local decisions control adoption; old systems have risks. Principle: Technological aid requires invitation.
