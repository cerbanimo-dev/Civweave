# Crossover writing protocol

Select two or three of the nine original worlds. Begin with a scene in one world's normal logic; introduce the second through evidence and consequence. Establish which materials, people, information and memories may cross. Preserve the separate laws of each world until characters deliberately negotiate a new rule. Avoid free infinite energy, unearned powers and automatic identity merges.

Suggested structures: stranger visits, reciprocal exchange, shared mystery, accident at a boundary, negotiated alliance, or documentary discovered in a different world. Keep characters free to refuse involvement. Include a limit on travel and a reversible test of each hypothesis.

Spoiler protection: use only public `entry` tier catalog data; hidden character identities, future romances, final scenes and private author chronology are never included. Model training is not a substitute for this input boundary.
