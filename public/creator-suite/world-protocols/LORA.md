# Optional storycraft LoRA — not a spoiler gate
Status: **specification only; not trained, downloaded or installed**.

For a compatible local inference runtime (e.g. a supported Qwen GGUF adapter path), prepare licensed, public, versioned training data from the nine original worlds. Use short positive/negative examples for: correct scene-local mechanics, believable crossovers, character-first genre jokes, suspenseful but fair clue placement, and serialized scene continuity. Maintain creator attribution and permissions.

Evaluate against held-out prompts: wrong-world physics, invented lore, forced tragic origins, unearned exposition, collapse of two worlds into one, excessive generic IP imitation, unwanted author-only revelations. Publish a dataset manifest with provenance and consent. Gate load on explicit user selection, device fit, runtime support, and offline availability. Keep the lore packets as the factual source of truth independent of adapter weights.

Do not claim that fine-tuning protects confidential secrets; exclusion and access control are the protection.
