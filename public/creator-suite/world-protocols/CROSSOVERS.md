# Crossover mechanics
Use any combination of the nine preinstalled universes, including all nine. Choose a primary scene location and an understandable crossing mechanism, such as portal, dimensional gutter, transit vessel, simulation handshake, dream boundary or repaired machine.

At each crossing:
1. Establish **where** the scene occurs and what laws are in force.
2. Show how abilities, technology, communication and consent work across the boundary.
3. Do not force harmonization. Conflicting rules can stay in conflict.
4. Give local inhabitants agency; do not treat a visiting protagonist as the owner of the world.
5. Make the intersection create a conflict or possibility that neither world can provide alone.
6. Keep genre-reference humor in character; explain the stakes through action rather than requiring outside fandom knowledge.
7. Preserve a readable continuity ledger: scene, worlds involved, crossing trigger, rule difference, consequence, open question.

The selection renderer can combine all nine. The active document editor remains responsible for saved scene revisions.
