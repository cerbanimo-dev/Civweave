What creation provenance means

What it can prove

When work is created inside Creator Suite, meaningful editing operations are recorded into a hash-chained local history. Civweave AI operations are recorded as AI operations when they happen. Human actions are recorded separately. Changing, removing, or reordering recorded events breaks verification.

What it does not claim

Creator Suite does not treat an AI detector as an oracle. Content imported without trustworthy creation history is marked External / Unknown, not accused of being AI generated. A review can identify anomalies or broken provenance, but it cannot turn uncertainty into proof.

Privacy

Detailed editing history is encrypted and stays on the creator's device by default. Public or Guild-visible receipts contain compact commitments and summaries rather than private drafts. If a creator participates in a Guild, the Guild may sample compact receipts under its bounded audit policy; sampling happens before any detailed packet is requested. Only selected matching packets are released, and the Cloudflare audit path keeps the extracted review evidence rather than retaining the raw decrypted packet.

Guild review

Clean cryptographic chains can be resolved mechanically. A Guild may permit a model to inspect structured provenance evidence for ambiguous routine cases, but the model is not given the creative work itself and is forbidden from inferring AI authorship from style. Severe anomalies and disputes go to independent human review. The creator cannot review their own case; independent reviewers see minimum necessary provenance evidence, not the creator's device identity, earlier findings, or earlier tribunal votes. A creator can file a signed appeal without rewriting the original creation history.

Labels

Human Created means the recorded origin contains human creation operations and no AI or unknown external origin. AI Generated or Mixed: AI + Human means platform AI is present in the recorded creation history. External / Unknown means reliable origin evidence is incomplete. Review labels such as Provenance Verified or Provenance Anomaly are shown separately because review is additive and never rewrites creation origin.

Independent verification

The Verify Provenance tool can inspect a Creator Suite receipt, additive review record, or encrypted provenance packet locally.

C2PA / Content Credentials

Civweave has a privacy-bounded mapping from its public receipt into C2PA actions and ingredient intent, but that mapping is not itself a Content Credential. C2PA export remains unavailable until the browser signing/WASM bundle passes the dependency-license audit, is vendored into the optional Creator Suite, and independently verifies signed, hard-bound exported assets. Creator Suite will not use a CDN or substitute unsigned JSON and call it a credential.
