What creation provenance means

Back to Creator Suite

What it can prove

When work is created inside Creator Suite, meaningful editing operations are recorded into a hash-chained local history. Civweave AI operations are recorded as AI operations when they happen. Human actions are recorded separately. Changing, removing, or reordering recorded events breaks verification.

What it does not claim

Creator Suite does not treat an AI detector as an oracle. Content imported without trustworthy creation history is marked External / Unknown, not accused of being AI generated. A review can identify anomalies or broken provenance, but it cannot turn uncertainty into proof.

Privacy

Detailed editing history stays on the creator's device by default. Public receipts contain compact commitments and summaries rather than private drafts. Detailed audit packets are encrypted before export or transport and should only be disclosed under an explicit review policy.

Labels

Human Created means the recorded origin contains human creation operations and no AI or unknown external origin. AI Generated or Mixed: AI + Human means platform AI is present in the recorded creation history. External / Unknown means reliable origin evidence is incomplete. Human review is additive and never rewrites the original creation history.

Independent verification

The Verify Provenance tool can inspect a Creator Suite receipt or encrypted provenance packet locally. Future C2PA and transparency-receipt adapters can carry compatible public claims outside Civweave without exposing private history.
